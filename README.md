## Google DeepMind Research Ready internship
During this 8-week internship I worked solo, with social workers and
Caerphilly Council, to tackle slow hospital discharge into social
care. Discharges were tracked on manual spreadsheets, and staff had to
visit wards for updates, so patients waited too long for the care they
needed.

My first version only summarised patient notes. On my supervisor's
advice, and after hearing social workers describe how poorly
information is coordinated, I rebuilt it as Pont AI (below), a system
that brings the information together in one place. I demonstrated the
working prototype to Caerphilly Council and to university colleagues,
including the head of computer science, and received positive
feedback. It is a prototype and has not been deployed in a hospital.

### Quran Memorisation App (solo project)
An Android app for students memorising the Quran. It covers all the
surahs, and students can mark their progress ayah by ayah. The app
sorts everything into memorised, due for revision and weak ayahs, so a
student always knows what is strong and what needs work. A home screen
shows a streak, daily-goal progress and recommendations on what to
study next, and a daily reminder prompts revision.

The main feature is voice recitation checking. A student records
themselves reciting, the app transcribes it, compares it with the
correct text word by word, and returns an accuracy percentage with the
mistakes highlighted. Students can also listen to recitations from a
choice of reciters, and the app works in light and dark themes.

**Tech:** Kotlin, Android, Hilt, Room, Retrofit, WorkManager

**Repository:**
[Quran Memorisation App](https://github.com/MAsif456/Qur-an-Memorisation-App)

### Accessible Games Platform (team project, team of 4)
A Steam-style website that lists only games accessible to people with
motor, dexterity and other disabilities, so the community can find
games they can actually play. Each game appears as a card with its
accessibility tags and categories. Clicking a card opens a full game
page showing all the key information, which disabilities the game
suits and its specific accessibility features, with a link to the
game's store page, since the platform does not sell games itself.
Users can read and post reviews and report reviews or games, and admins
review and resolve those reports in an admin panel.

**My role:** built the individual game information pages, the keyboard
navigation and skip link, the review and reporting features with the
admin panel, the Jest tests and the CI/CD pipeline.

**Tech:** MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js), TypeScript, Jest, TypeDoc,
GitLab CI/CD

**Repository:**
[Accessible Games Platform](https://github.com/MAsif456/Accessible-Games-Platform)
